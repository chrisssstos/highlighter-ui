Industrial-look assets for HighlighterUI.

brushed.png and grain.png are derived from ambientCG scans Metal009 and Metal011
(https://ambientcg.com), released under CC0 1.0 (public domain).

knob.png, screw.png, sheen.png, vignette.png and scanlines.png are rendered by
tools/render_assets.py and the kit itself.
