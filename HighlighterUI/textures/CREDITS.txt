Classic-skin assets for HighlighterUI, made for the kit:
knob_classic.png and sheen.png are rendered by tools/render_assets.py.
No third-party graphics.

Icons (HighlighterUI/ParamIconPaths.js): the path data for ParamIcon's
action icons (undo, redo, copy, add, delete, save, saveas (save_as),
restore (settings_backup_restore), load (file_open), fullscreen,
duplicate, clear) is from Material
Symbols, Sharp style, filled, 24 px: copyright Google LLC, licensed under
the Apache License, Version 2.0
(https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).
Source: https://github.com/google/material-design-icons
The transport and animation icons (play, pause, loop, pencil, the moving
ball and the rest) are drawn for the kit.

kit's author for the title; the file carries no licence of its own.
